The Honest Woodcutter
Ek chhote se gaon me ek imaandar aur mehnati lakadhara rehta tha jiska naam Ramu tha. Wo roz subah jaldi uthkar jungle jaata aur lakdi kaat kar bazaar me bechta tha. Isi se wo apne parivaar ka pet palta tha. Ek din wo nadi ke kinare ped kaat raha tha tabhi achanak uski kulhadi haath se fisal gayi aur seedha gehri nadi me gir gayi. Ramu bahut pareshaan ho gaya kyunki uske paas sirf wahi ek kulhadi thi. Wo nadi ke kinare baith kar rone laga aur sochne laga ki ab wo apna kaam kaise karega. Tabhi achanak ek devi wahan prakat hui aur unhone pucha ki tum kyun ro rahe ho. Ramu ne imaandari se sab kuch bata diya. Devi nadi me gayi aur ek chamakti hui sone ki kulhadi lekar aayi aur boli kya ye tumhari hai. Ramu ne turant mana kar diya aur bola nahi ye meri nahi hai. Phir devi chandi ki kulhadi lekar aayi aur phir pucha kya ye tumhari hai. Ramu ne phir mana kar diya aur kaha nahi ye bhi meri nahi hai. Aakhir me devi uski purani lohe ki kulhadi lekar aayi jise dekhte hi Ramu khush ho gaya aur bola haan ye meri hi kulhadi hai. Devi uski imaandari aur sachai se bahut prabhavit hui aur usse inaam ke roop me sone aur chandi ki kulhadi bhi de di. Ramu ne devi ka dhanyavaad kiya aur khushi khushi apne ghar laut gaya. Us din ke baad wo aur bhi zyada imaandari aur mehnat se kaam karne laga aur gaon ke log bhi uski tarif karne lage.
Moral: Imaandari aur sachai hamesha safalta aur khushi dilati hai.
